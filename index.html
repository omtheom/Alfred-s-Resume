About me

I have spent the last four years on Qatar's front line of guest and worker safety. What started as a security guard post with European Guarding and Security Co. grew into rotational lifeguard deployments at four of the country's most demanding sites: Katara Beach Club, The Torch Hotel, Aspire, and Ras Laffan Port. Supervisors don't hand out placements like that lightly — they go to the guards you can trust with a five-star pool deck and a strategic energy port in the same rotation.

While I was on the ground I was also studying. I earned my Diploma in Nautical Studies at Bandari College in Mombasa — SOLAS, the ISPS Code, ship security planning and passenger vessel safety. In 2026 I completed my Ship Security Officer certification at Bandari Maritime Academy, along with Crowd Management & Passenger Safety and Crisis Management & Human Behaviour at the Technical University of Mombasa. My STCW Basic Safety Training has been with me since 2017.

I hold my Seaman Book. My passport is valid and I am ready to commit to 4- to 6-month contract rotations, with a C1/D visa to be obtained on contract award. I speak Swahili and English well and Arabic at a working level — and I have started Spanish, French and German, because a passenger deck rarely speaks one language.

Away from the deck I read, travel, and keep a personal practice of long-distance swimming.
