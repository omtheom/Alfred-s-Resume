About me

I have spent nine years working at pool decks and on the shoreline. I started in Mombasa, where the Indian Ocean taught me how strong water can be. I earned my first lifesaving certificate from the Royal Life Saving Society in 2016, my Personal Survival Award from the Amateur Swimming Association the year after, and my drowning prevention training in 2021.

I spent three of those years at Katara Beach Club in Doha — one of the top waterfront spots in the Gulf. There I learned to watch closely without getting in the way, the way fine hotels expect. In early 2025 I came back to the pool deck at the 21 Street Hotel, where I now run the pool each day and help keep the safety culture strong.

Swahili is my first language. I speak English well and Arabic at a working level. That mix has helped me along the Kenyan coast and across the Gulf. When I am off duty I read, travel, and swim long distances on my own time.
